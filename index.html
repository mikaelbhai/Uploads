Forbidden times for nafl prayer

No voluntary prayer during these windows

  • 1
    After praying Fajr
    until sunrise
  • 2
    Sunrise
    until the sun is a spear's length up (~15 min)
  • 3
    Sun at its zenith
    a few minutes before Dhuhr, until it passes
  • 4
    After praying Asr
    until the sun begins to set
  • 5
    While the sun is setting
    until it has fully set
Still allowed: making up a missed fard, Janazah, two rak'ahs after tawaf, and repeating a prayer with the congregation. Ibn Taymiyyah, Ibn Baz and Ibn Uthaymeen also allow prayers with a cause, like tahiyyat al-masjid; the Hanbali madhhab does not.
Dhuhr: the Hanbali madhhab counts 10 confirmed rak'ahs daily (2 before Dhuhr, hadith of Ibn Umar). Ibn Baz and Ibn Uthaymeen teach 12 (4 before Dhuhr, hadith of Umm Habibah).
Witr: the most emphasized sunnah in the Hanbali school; best in the last third of the night.
